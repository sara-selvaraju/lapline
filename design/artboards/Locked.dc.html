Harbourfront Race, on now

The race is live, but live data isn't switched on

OpenF1 only serves data during a session to sponsors. Without sponsor access, this race opens for free replay from 1:30 am Monday.

How to turn on live data

Sponsor OpenF1 for €9.90 a month, then add the login to the server's settings on Railway as OPENF1_USERNAME and OPENF1_PASSWORD. Live data starts within a minute.
